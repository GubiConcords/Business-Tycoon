# Business Tycoon

A browser business simulator. You start with **$5,000** and build an empire any way you like. It's plain HTML/CSS/JS with no build step or dependencies: open `index.html` in a browser.

## Ways to make money

| Route | What you do |
|---|---|
| 💼 **9–5 job** | Clock in and click **WORK**. Each click is one minute of your shift (9 AM–5 PM = 480 clicks), and the game clock pauses while you're at the office. Pay starts at **1¢ per click**, and promotions based on career clicks take you from Intern to Vice President at **40¢ per click** |
| 📈 **Stocks** | 14 companies across 9 sectors, with daily price moves, news events, crashes, rallies and quarterly dividends |
| 🪙 **Crypto** | 6 volatile coins, including a meme coin that can moon or get rugged |
| 🧪 **Startups** | Angel-invest in pitches. Most fail, a few return 10–60× |
| 🍽️ **Restaurants** | Food truck → café → diner → sushi → fine dining → burger franchise. Hire and pay cooks, waiters, cleaners and managers. Set prices and ingredient quality, run marketing, renovate, or work shifts yourself |
| 🏭 **Factories & R&D** | Commodity plants (plastic, steel, aluminum, wire, wafers, glass, batteries, cups, toys) feed a **Design Studio** where you create your own products and pay R&D to develop them: **chips** (28nm→3nm, Efficient / Balanced / Super chip, 2–16 cores), **car engines** (I4, V6, V8, electric), **jet engines** (15k–100k lbf; turbojet, turbofan or geared), **cars**, **phones** and **aircraft** (50–600 seats, range, twin or four engines). Use your own parts or buy off-the-shelf ones (stock engines, licensed turbofans, generic chips). Most products need **certification** before production (FAA type certificate for aircraft, FAA engine certification, safety & emissions, FCC), and certification can fail. Each design needs its own production line, and the chip fab must be upgraded for smaller process nodes. Prices drop as you flood the market with one design, so a range of designs sells best |
| 🤝 **Deals & diplomacy** | Sign **supply contracts** with other companies (deliver goods or your own designed products on schedule, above market price), bid on **government tenders** from six fictional countries, run **AI service contracts**, start **joint ventures** that track a partner's stock, and build relations with business lunches, trade missions and liaison offices. **Trade agreements** cut commodity prices (up to 15%). Missed deliveries earn strikes and penalties, and political crises can void government deals overnight |
| 🤖 **AI Lab** | Open an AI Lab HQ, hire **software engineers, ML researchers and data engineers** (they produce research points), license training data, and open **data centers** (server room → hyperscale campus, rented or bought, with big power and cooling bills). Fill them with **GPU servers** or servers **built from your own chip designs**. **Train models** from Tiny to Frontier, sell an AI product to users, take AI contracts and, once capable enough, use your AI **as your own employees**: it works your 9–5 shifts and replaces restaurant, scrapyard and factory staff and even your AI researchers |
| 🎖️ **Defense** | With a **Defense manufacturing license** (from the Legal tab), design and build **helicopters, tanks, fighter jets (4th gen → 5th gen stealth) and bombers**. Each one is assembled from parts you design yourself or buy off the shelf: **avionics, radar, armor, rotor systems** and engines. The same parts work in your own airliners and private jets. Military designs pass **military acceptance trials**, and governments order them through the Deals tab |
| 🚀 **Space agency** | Found an agency (spaceport + launch site license), design **rocket engines** (kerolox/methalox/hydrolox, expendable or reusable), **rockets** (1 t → 100 t to orbit, reusable boosters), **satellites** and **crew capsules**, then launch: build an income-earning **satellite constellation**, fly **commercial payloads** and **space tourists**, and chase milestones: **space station, Moon landing, Mars**. Includes mission control staff, extra launch pads, pad turnaround, launch insurance and real failure risk |
| ⚖️ **Legal & advisors** | Retain a law firm (local → elite) and hire in-house counsel, a compliance officer, patent attorney, regulatory affairs team, lobbyist and PR agency. They raise lawsuit win odds, cut fines and penalties (audits, accidents, contract strikes, police) by up to 40%, speed certifications, make permits cheaper, block copycat products, improve relations and reputation, and unlock the defense license |
| ⚡ **Energy** | Build solar, wind, hydro, gas, coal and nuclear plants, plus fusion reactors after a 3-stage research program. Plants are rented or bought, take days to build and suffer outages. Your own power supplies your businesses first (saving the retail price), and the surplus is sold to the grid at a fluctuating wholesale price plus renewable subsidies. Drill **oil wells** (crude for your refineries) and **gas fields** (free fuel for your gas plants), add **grid storage** built from your own battery cells, and retrofit **carbon capture** |
| 🌱 **CO₂ emissions** | Every factory, fossil plant, kilowatt bought from the grid, kitchen, rocket launch, private-jet trip, cargo ship and even your lifestyle emits CO₂, broken down by source. Above a weekly free allowance you pay the government's **carbon price**. Buy, bank and sell **carbon credits**, plant **forests** and build **direct air capture**. Your **ESG score** moves your share price, green governments' tenders and investor mood |
| 🌍 **World & politics** | Random **global events**: pandemic, war, recession, oil shock, tech boom, housing crash. Your home country is run by one of four parties whose **policies** (corporate tax, carbon price, minimum wage, renewable subsidy, defense budget, AI regulation, permit costs) change the whole economy. Fund parties in **elections**, or **run for office** yourself from city council to **President**, where you set national policy. Beware conflict-of-interest scandals (a blind trust helps). Foreign governments hold elections too |
| 🥊 **Rival tycoons** | Four AI tycoons grow their empires on a **leaderboard**. They launch competing products, open restaurants next to yours, poach unhappy executives, sue you, outbid you on tenders and make buyout offers. Hold peace summits, sue them back, or **acquire** them |
| 🏛️ **Corporate** | Found a **holding company**, then **IPO** it. Your share price tracks net worth, operating profit, ESG and market mood. Pay dividends, buy back or sell more shares, and survive quarterly **shareholder meetings**. Launch friendly or hostile **takeovers** of the 14 listed companies, and found your own **bank** (deposits, lending policies, loan defaults, bank runs) |
| 🚢 **Logistics & exports** | Buy trucks, freight trains, cargo ships and cargo planes, and **export** goods and your own products to six countries at higher prices. You deal with tariffs (trade agreements remove them), transit times, port strikes, piracy and cargo insurance. Arms exports need the defense license |
| 👔 **Executives** | Hire named division chiefs (Restaurants, Manufacturing, Real Estate, Energy, AI & Tech, Space & Defense, CFO, Logistics) with skill, salary, loyalty and traits. Good chiefs **run their division**: they auto-staff kitchens, advertise vacancies, boost output and cut costs. Keep them loyal or rivals poach them. **Unions** may organize your factories |
| 📺 **Media empire** | Newspaper, magazine, radio, TV, a streaming service (with a content budget) and a social network (needs AI moderation). They earn ads and subscriptions, and build **influence** for elections, offices and reputation. Optional self-promotion props up your product prices |
| 🚗 **Scrapyard** | Buy junk cars at auction, crush them for metal and parts, or repair and flip them. Rare barn finds show up now and then |
| 🏘️ **Real estate** | Buy property, set rent levels, keep tenants happy, renovate, evict, or develop new buildings from scratch |
| 🛩️ **Garage & hangar** | Buy personal **cars** (hatchback → hypercar, classics appreciate) and **private aircraft** (ultralight → VIP airliner), or take **your own car and aircraft designs** off the production line. Cars need garage space (depends on your home, or buy a car vault), and aircraft need a **hangar** at the airport (rent or buy) plus a **pilot license** (flight school) or a hired crew. Vehicles cost insurance, fuel and maintenance, but give **prestige** (better tender odds and business lunches), **comfort** (more max energy), getaway trips that restore energy, and, with a flyable jet, cheaper and more frequent trade missions |
| 🏎️ **Racing** | Buy and tune cars and enter races, from track days to the Grand Prix. Bet on yourself or hire a pro driver. Underground races pay more but can get raided by police |
| 🎰 **Casino** | Plinko (3 risk levels), slots, roulette, blackjack, crash, dice and a weekly lottery |
| 🏦 **Bank** | Loans with a credit limit tied to your net worth, plus a savings account |
| 🏢 **Running costs** | Opening any business costs **equipment + a permit/license** (from a $400 food vendor license to a $5M FAA production certificate) **+ premises**: rent them (30-day deposit plus daily rent) or buy them outright (no rent, small property tax, value follows the housing market). You can buy rented premises later. Every day you pay **electricity, water, gas, internet & phone, waste disposal and insurance**, scaled to how busy the business is. Utility prices swing with heatwaves, droughts and pipeline outages. Insurance covers most of the cost of fires and accidents. Race cars cost garage space and insurance, landlords pay property tax and utilities on vacant units, and you pay your own **living costs** (parents' basement → mansion), which set your daily energy |
| 🧾 **Taxes & risk** | Business profits are taxed weekly. Pay honestly, use creative accounting, or set up an offshore shell company. Each step down that path raises your audit risk, and audits are expensive |

Also included: energy-based hustles, random events (health inspectors, critics, viral posts, fires, lawsuits, inheritances), 29 achievements, a net worth chart, an event log and autosave to `localStorage`.

## Deeper systems
- **Restaurants:** FoodDash delivery partnership, chain branding across locations of one type, signature dishes, Michelin-style stars (up to 3), and rival restaurants opening next door.
- **Real estate:** five districts that gentrify or decline, **mortgages**, zoning fights when you develop, and big towers built **phase by phase** (finished floors rent out early).
- **AI:** AI safety researchers, AI incidents (data leaks, bad advice) that cost fines, users and public trust, open-source competitors, AI regulation from the government, and defense AI contracts.

## Your dynasty, goals and quality of life
- **Difficulty** (Easy/Normal/Hard) and **scenarios**: Sandbox, Billion in 1,500 days, Green Tycoon, The Comeback, King of the Hill.
- **Aging & heirs:** one game year is 180 days. From 65 your character can die. Retire or pass on to one of three heirs with traits, pay inheritance tax (a family trust cuts it), and earn **legacy points** that boost future games.
- **Goals & guided mode:** a 20-step goal chain with cash rewards. Guided mode reveals tabs as you progress, so new players aren't overwhelmed.
- **Inbox, "needs attention" panel and timeline**, plus **stats & history** (wealth breakdown now vs 30/90 days ago, lifetime stats).
- **Save slots**, **export/import** of save files, notification filtering and optional **sound**.

## Controls

- **Speed buttons** (top right) or **Space** to pause or resume. **N** advances one day.
- You get **100⚡ energy** per day for hands-on work (hustles, restaurant shifts, scrapyard work, driving races).
- Going bankrupt (net worth below −$50K with no cash) ends the game.
